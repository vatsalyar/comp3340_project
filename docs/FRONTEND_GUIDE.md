# Front-End & User Documentation
**COMP3340 Civic Parts Depot**

Welcome to the COMP3340 Civic Parts Depot! This application is a fully interactive e-commerce platform specifically scoped for aftermarket parts for the 2008 Honda Civic EX-L 2dr Coupe.

## 1. Customizing Your Experience (Themes)
The application features three distinct, responsive layout themes to suit your preference. 
* Locate the **Theme Selector** dropdown in the top-right corner of the header.
* Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or **Blue** themes.
* The site utilizes CSS variables to instantly transition the color scheme and layout across all pages. Your preference will be saved as you navigate the site.

## 2. Browsing & Features
* **Catalog:** Browse over 20 unique products divided into categories (Aero & Body, Maintenance, Interior, Wheel & Tire).
* **Data Visualization:** View a dynamically generated, interactive SVG bar chart displaying our inventory distribution by category.
* **Currency Converter:** Use the live API tool to convert USD pricing into CAD before making a purchase decision.
* **Interactive Map:** View our distribution center location via the embedded map tool.

## 3. Creating an Account
To utilize the full features of the site, click **Register** in the navigation menu. Once registered, you will gain access to a personalized dashboard where you can:
* Place orders for parts.
* View your entire **Order History**.
* Submit and track **Service Requests** for fitment help or returns.

## 4. Placing an Order
1. Navigate to the **Catalog** and click on a product to view its details.
2. Ensure you are logged in to your account.
3. Review the specific fitment data to ensure compatibility with your vehicle.
4. Select your preferred tier (Standard or Premium).
5. Click **Order this Part**. You will be issued a unique Order Reference Number (e.g., `ORD-A1B2C3D4`).
6. You can track the real-time status of this reference number using the **Track Order** feature in your Profile.
